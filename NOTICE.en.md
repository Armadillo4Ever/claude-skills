# Attributions

## skills/raisonnement-juridique-administratif

Derived from the skill `raisonnement-juridique` by Amaury Fouret, published under the MIT licence
in https://github.com/lawve-ai/awesome-legal-skills and devoted to civil litigation.

Elements retained: the rule requiring the full text to be retrieved before citation,
the distinction between what calls for an answer from the judge and what does not,
the skeleton of the syllogism, the principle of an explicit hierarchy of decisions.

Elements set aside: the Code of Civil Procedure, the structure of the civil judgment,
the taxonomy of the Court of Cassation (*Cour de cassation*), the numerical authority coefficients,
the dependence on external MCP tools.

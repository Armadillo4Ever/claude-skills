# Advisory review of a draft text

French sources are quoted in free translation; the French text prevails.

This skill conducts the legal review of a French draft normative text. It follows the order in which the questions arise, from the most radical to the finest: a provision adopted by an authority lacking competence need not be examined on the merits.

## Scope, and what this skill does not do

It applies to bills (*projets de loi*), private members' bills (*propositions de loi*), draft ordinances, decrees and ministerial or prefectoral orders (*arrêtés*).

It does not draft the opinion of an advisory institution and does not imitate its form: it conducts the review and reports its results. The form of the opinions, notes and reports of a given institution is a matter for that institution.

It does not apply to litigation: the review of a draft and the adjudication of a dispute follow neither the same office nor the same order.

## Mandatory rule on sources

Never cite a text, a decision or a paragraph of the grounds without having retrieved its full text, in the version in force at the relevant date. A truncated excerpt taken from a search result is not sufficient.

Never cite from memory a decision number, a date or an article reference. If the exact reference has not been found, say so.

The sources of the review are public: the Constitution, the texts in force on Légifrance, the case law of the Constitutional Council (*Conseil constitutionnel*), of the Council of State (*Conseil d'État*), of the Court of Justice of the European Union and of the European Court of Human Rights, the Legislative Drafting Guide (*Guide de légistique*), and the opinions delivered on bills that are published.

## The order of review

### 1. Competence of the author and level of norm

Does the subject matter fall within the domain of statute, article 34 of the Constitution, or of regulation, article 37? A regulatory provision inserted in a bill is not irregular in itself, but it calls for an observation; the converse, a legislative provision adopted by decree, is a lack of competence.

Check the signing authority, the countersignature, and, for a decree, whether it must be adopted after consultation of the Council of State (*décret en Conseil d'État*) or in the Council of Ministers. An error on this point vitiates the entire act.

### 2. Legal basis and enabling provision

For an implementing decree: does the enabling provision exist, and does the text remain within its limits? In the absence of an express enabling provision, the general regulatory power, article 21 of the Constitution, may suffice, but it does not allow encroachment on the domain of statute.

For an ordinance, article 38: the scope of the enabling provision, its duration, and the time limit for tabling the ratification bill. An ordinance that exceeds its enabling provision, even by one word, is irregular on that point.

### 3. Mandatory prior consultations

List the consultations required by the texts, check that they took place, that they concerned the text actually submitted and not an earlier substantially different version, and that the time limits were complied with. The omission of a mandatory consultation is a procedural irregularity affecting the legality of the act.

For a bill, also check the impact assessment, required by the organic law adopted on the basis of article 39 of the Constitution.

### 4. Compliance with the Constitution

Go through the relevant reference norms: the rights and freedoms guaranteed by the Constitution, the principle of equality and the differences in treatment it allows, freedom of enterprise, the right of property, freedom of expression, the guarantee of rights and legal certainty, non-retroactivity in punitive matters, the legality of offences and penalties, the objective of constitutional value of accessibility and intelligibility of the law.

Check the absence of negative incompetence (*incompétence négative*): the legislature must exhaust its competence and not refer to the regulatory power what it is for the legislature to lay down.

For a private member's bill or an amendment, examine the link with the text under discussion and, where relevant, the rules of financial admissibility.

### 5. Compatibility with international commitments and Union law

European Convention on Human Rights and its protocols; international conventions applicable to the subject matter; Union law.

For a transposition: fidelity to the directive, without under-transposition or unjustified over-transposition, the latter having in any event to be assumed and reasoned. Check, depending on the subject matter, the procedural obligations specific to Union law, in particular the prior notification of technical rules and the State aid regime.

### 6. Articulation with existing law

Does the text fit in correctly? List the provisions to be coordinated, the consequential repeals, the cross-references that have become wrong, the duplications created. A provision that is correct in itself may render the neighbouring article incoherent.

### 7. Legislative drafting quality

Drafting of amendments, accuracy of designations and cross-references, counting of paragraphs (*alinéas*), definitions, absence of non-normative provisions. See the legislative drafting skill when it comes to reading, writing or applying an amending instruction.

### 8. Application in the overseas territories

Determine, territory by territory, whether the regime is that of legislative identity or that of speciality, and whether an express mention of applicability is necessary. Check the updating of the applicability counters (*compteurs d'applicabilité*) where the amended text contains them. This is the most frequent and the most easily avoidable oversight.

### 9. Entry into force and transitional provisions

Date of entry into force, fate of ongoing situations, of pending procedures and of authorisations granted, necessary implementing measures and a realistic time limit for adopting them, articulation with the repealed texts.

### 10. Effects and implementation

Is the text applicable in fact? New burdens, resources, time limits, effective sanctions, articulation with existing information systems and procedures. This question is not a legal one, but an inapplicable text is a failed text.

## Three outcomes, and a distinction to be maintained

For each provision examined, one conclusion among three: it may be retained as it stands; it calls for an amendment, the wording of which must be proposed; it cannot be retained.

Distinguish rigorously what is **legally irregular**, and must be set aside or corrected, from what is **inexpedient or perfectible**, which is a matter for an observation. Presenting the second category as the first is an error of analysis that discredits the whole.

## Questions of principle

Flag separately the questions that go beyond the text under review: those that call for an arbitration, those whose solution will serve as a precedent, those on which the state of the law is uncertain. Formulate them as questions, briefly, with the terms of the alternative and the elements that make it possible to decide.

## Frequent traps

- Examining the substance of a provision before having checked competence and legal basis.
- Taking for granted a consultation of which nothing establishes that it concerned the text submitted.
- Checking the compliance of an amending text without reading the consolidated text that will result from it.
- Forgetting the overseas territories and the transitional provisions, which come at the end of the review and get lost when time is short.
- Citing a decision from memory, or attributing to it a scope it does not have.
- Confusing the opinion delivered on a draft with the judicial decision: they have neither the same object nor the same authority.

## Checks before delivering

- Every point of the grid has been gone through, and the points that do not apply are explicitly stated as such.
- Every irregularity noted is linked to a precise norm, cited in its applicable version and verified.
- Every proposed amendment is drafted, and not merely described.
- What is a matter of irregularity and what is a matter of expediency are separated.
- The questions of principle are isolated and formulated as such.
